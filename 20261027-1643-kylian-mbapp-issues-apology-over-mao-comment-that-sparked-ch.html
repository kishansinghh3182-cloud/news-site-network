Kylian Mbappé Issues Apology Over Mao Comment That Sparked Chinese Outrage

27 Oct 2026 · Source: BBC News

French football sensation Kylian Mbappé has issued a public apology following a wave of severe criticism from internet users in China. The controversy arose after the athlete used the term dictator to describe Mao Zedong, the historical leader of China, during a media discussion centered around viral internet memes.

The remarks were made while the prominent sports figure was participating in a segment focused on online culture and humorous digital content. During the conversation, the former Chinese leader was brought up, prompting the player to use the controversial label. This characterization quickly spread across digital platforms, leading to an intense negative reaction from the public within the nation.

For many everyday followers and consumers, public figures addressing sensitive historical figures carries significant weight, especially when dealing with international audiences. Sports stars often walk a fine line between casual commentary and political diplomacy, as their words can easily cross borders and offend cultural sensibilities. The backlash highlights how quickly offhand remarks during media appearances can damage a celebrity's standing in vital global markets.

Observers will likely monitor how this incident impacts the athlete's future international engagements, brand partnerships, and public relations strategies moving forward.

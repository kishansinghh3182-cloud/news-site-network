Inside Rosebank: Life Aboard an Untouched Offshore Energy Reserve

25 Oct 2026 · Source: BBC News

The Rosebank reserve is an untouched offshore oil development currently situated at the heart of a heated political conflict. With the future of this unworked deposit hanging in the balance, Scotland editor James Cook traveled to the site to uncover how workers experience everyday routines on an offshore vessel. His reporting offers a clear view into human existence stationed at an energy resource that has yet to undergo active extraction.

Documenting circumstances aboard this ship explains why the standoff holds significance beyond national policy debates. As the untouched field waits to learn its ultimate destiny, personnel stationed over the reservoir endure an ongoing state of suspension. This insight reveals how high-level political friction directly influences practical operations at dormant energy projects, demonstrating the tangible human and operational consequences when decisions over natural resources remain stalled.

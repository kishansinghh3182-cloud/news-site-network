SpaceX Prepares Simultaneous Recovery Attempt for Starship Components

13 Nov 2026 · Source: BBC News

SpaceX is aiming to retrieve both halves of its massive Starship launch system in a single operation for the very first time. Elon Musk's aerospace enterprise plans to capture the towering booster and the spacecraft simultaneously during an upcoming flight test. This ambitious maneuver marks a major operational milestone as the company pushes the boundaries of its reusable rocket technology.

The Starship architecture is designed as a fully reusable transportation system capable of carrying large amounts of cargo and crew to Earth orbit, the Moon, and eventually Mars. To achieve rapid reusability, SpaceX developed specialized mechanical infrastructure to pluck the hardware directly out of the sky upon its return. Successfully executing this dual recovery would demonstrate unprecedented precision in catching both the primary booster stage and the upper-flight vehicle.

Mastering this synchronized recovery technique is vital for dramatically lowering the cost of spaceflight and increasing launch frequency. By avoiding ocean splashdowns and preserving the expensive flight hardware, the company can refurbish and relaunch components much faster. Proving that both sections can be safely secured in one flight brings the engineering team significantly closer to their ultimate vision of routine interplanetary travel.

Observers and aerospace enthusiasts can follow future updates from the company to see when the flight test window opens and whether the dual-catch attempt succeeds.

Kellogg Drops Rice from Rice Krispies Due to Climate Disruptions

04 Nov 2026 · Source: BBC News

Breakfast table staples are getting a surprise makeover as Kellogg alters the recipe for Rice Krispies by swapping out traditional cereal grains for wheat-based ingredients, driven by environmental shifts impacting harvests. Consumers purchasing the familiar boxes will soon notice an updated allergen notice on the packaging to alert anyone sensitive to wheat gluten.

This unexpected shift highlights how shifting weather patterns and climate conditions are forcing food manufacturers to rethink their supply chains and ingredient sourcing. For decades, the famous cereal relied on classic grains, but agricultural challenges have made securing consistent supplies more difficult and costly.

For everyday shoppers, this transition means more than just a subtle tweak to the morning routine; it underscores how global agricultural pressures directly affect grocery store shelves. When climate events disrupt traditional crop yields, food companies must adapt their formulations to keep products available, which often introduces new dietary considerations for households.

Shoppers may want to keep an eye on grocery store shelves and product labels to see how recipes continue to evolve in response to ongoing agricultural shifts.

Eric Bauza, Renowned Voice Behind Iconic Animated Characters, Passes Away at 46

26 Oct 2026 · Source: BBC News

The entertainment industry is mourning the loss of Eric Bauza, the celebrated voice actor famous for bringing classic animated personalities like Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck to life, who has died at the age of 46. Throughout his career, Bauza became a familiar fixture in modern animation by stepping into some of the most historic cartoon roles in television and film history.

Born into a generation that grew up watching these legendary Looney Tunes characters, Bauza eventually earned the responsibility of portraying them for contemporary audiences. His vocal talents extended well beyond the famous rabbit and duck, establishing him as a versatile performer capable of adapting to numerous cartoon personas across various studio projects.

His recent work included lending his voice to a variety of roles in the summer box office success Coyote vs. Acme. This high-profile project highlighted his ongoing relevance and enduring popularity within the animation sector, demonstrating his ability to contribute to major cinematic releases that resonated with both children and adults.

Observers and fans of the animation industry will likely monitor upcoming studio announcements to see how productions handle future voice casting for these enduring characters.
